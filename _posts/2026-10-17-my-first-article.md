## The Customer Journey in Automotive Digital Marketing

Car buyers rarely make every decision in a single interaction. They may discover a vehicle on social media, research it on Google, compare dealership websites, and eventually submit an inquiry or visit a showroom.

A successful digital marketing strategy supports customers at every stage of this journey.

| Customer Journey Stage | Marketing Strategy | Primary Objective |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Social media, video advertising, display campaigns | Introduce the dealership and its vehicles |
| Research | SEO, vehicle pages, informative content | Help customers explore their options |
| Consideration | Search ads, retargeting, vehicle comparisons | Encourage inquiries and dealership engagement |
| Conversion | Landing pages, test-drive forms, lead campaigns | Generate qualified leads and bookings |
| Follow-up | CRM workflows, email marketing, relevant reminders | Support ongoing customer communication |

By connecting these activities, iPopi Agency helps dealerships create a more consistent digital experience from initial discovery to sales inquiry.

## Measuring Success Beyond Clicks and Impressions

A campaign can generate thousands of website visits without producing enough qualified leads. For this reason, automotive digital marketing should be evaluated using metrics that reflect dealership performance.

For example, a dealership might compare two campaigns. One generates a large number of inexpensive inquiries, while another produces fewer inquiries but a higher percentage of test-drive bookings and vehicle sales.

The second campaign may deliver greater business value despite generating fewer leads.

By connecting advertising data with lead management and sales information, dealerships can better understand which channels and campaigns contribute to meaningful outcomes.

At iPopi Agency, we believe digital marketing performance should be evaluated in the context of real dealership objectives, not just surface-level engagement metrics.
